04 / A little more clarity

Before we
get started.

Can we start with a single project?

Yes. A focused campaign, landing page or creative brief can be a useful starting point. The scope should match your immediate goal, with room to build from there.

How is the right marketing mix decided?

Start with your audience, business goals and budget. These shape the recommended channels, messaging and measurement plan, rather than using the same formula for every brand.

What should we include in our brief?

A short introduction to your business, your main objective, target audience, timeline and an indicative budget. Any existing brand assets or campaign learnings are helpful too.

How do you measure progress?

Agree on the measures before a campaign starts. Depending on your objective, these might include qualified enquiries, acquisition cost, conversion rate or audience engagement.
